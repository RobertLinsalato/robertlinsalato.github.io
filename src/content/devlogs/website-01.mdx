## /// GOODBYE_3D_SHOWCASE
One of the more difficult decisions I had to make was removing the 3D Showcase from the site. There's a few reasons, but it mainly came down to:
1) It had lots of storage and computational issues, even with the compressed GLTF files
2) It didn't work well/at all on mobile
3) There really wasn't a big use for it.

Obviously there's always a chance to bring it back in the future, but it's not something that's on the top of my list now. If Sketchfab ever goes down, I'll definitely dust off the 'ol ThreeJS side of my brain.

## /// CLEANING+UTILITIES
After a break from working on it, I decided to take a look through my website's code that I hadn't touched since making it.
Throughout my whole site, I kept on making rookie mistakes and creating new classes when existing ones worked just fine/I just forgot about them.
A huge thing that I completely forgot about till recently was how the site appears on mobile devices, so I went through and changed things where needed so there wasn't annoying overhang.
I also added a sitemap... not really that important for a site of my size, but it wouldn't hurt.
Also a bunch of other things, but they're not really something I can show visually, though all of it was definitely needed with the amount I have going on. 

## /// WRAPPING_UP
After another month of working on my site, my love of Astro's ecosystem was reinforced.
In the beginning, I was enamored with its ability to work so fast and keep everything so compact.
The more I use it, the more I love the workflow and sometimes it reminds me of working in game engines.

It's likely this will be a pretty rare devlog to be updated, just because I'm very comfortable with the place my site's in right now, unless I figure out a way to break everything.
