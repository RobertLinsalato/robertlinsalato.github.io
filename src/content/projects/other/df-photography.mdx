# BACKGROUND
Photography and videography have always been an interest of mine. 
Headshot photography was always something I dabbled in but never fully tried because I was always too busy with other projects.
For <i><a href="/projects/games/knightfall" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Knightfall</a></i>, I took photos of the whole team (which didn't end up being posted on social media). 
I wanted to do the same with my cohort and give everyone professional looking photos that would stand out against other people.

# SETUP
I wanted to make sure the lighting added definition and depth to the photos, which is partially why I added colored lights to the plan.
Before photo day, I prepped by remaking APU's Cinema Lab with basic primatives in Blender from a reference photo.
The lighting setup was as follows:
- __*Key & Fill*__ - 2 GVM Light Panels
- __*Rim*__ - Aputure Nova P300 w/ 1/2 Stop Net
- __*Subject L & R*__ - 2x Aputure MC Mini Panels per side

After prepping different looks for the team, photo day arrived.
I photographed everyone on a Canon R6 with a 105mm adjustable lens.
Each person customized their own lighitng looks so each one was unique. 

# FINAL EXPORTS
With some touchups in Affinity, the photos were completed and exported 2 days later.
Along with the photos, I rendered overlays with everyone's roles for social media posting.
